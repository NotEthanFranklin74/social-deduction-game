# Game Rules:

## How many players can join:

Minimum: 6\
Maximum: 15 (for now)

## How many Traitors are assigned: 

Minimum: 1\
Maximum: Number of players DIV 5

This is decided by the Host at the start of the game.

## How are Traitors assigned:

Randomly picked by the computer at the start of the game

## When can players be banished:

At the end of a round of a game and after the round table discussion

## When can players be murdered:

After the round table and banishment of a player

## How does voting and ties work:

Voting is done where each player selects another player on their phone screen, they then turn their devices round one by one to reveal who they have voted for. When the host decides, the player is banished and the round continues.

Ties are managed by revote, everyone is asked to pick again between the two characters in the tie. If another tie is reached, a player is chosen at random to be banished.

## Conditions for winning:

### Traitors:

The Traitors win if the number of traitors left in the game equals or exceeds the number of Faithful left after a banishment.

### Faithful:

The Faithful win if the Traitors are all eliminated after a banishment.

## What does the Host control:

The Host's primary role is to keep order in the game.

They set the initial rules when setting up a game, including: 

 - Length of Round Table Discussion
 - Number of Traitors (within constraints)
